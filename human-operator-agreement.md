## Role terms

### OP1 — Identity, scope and actual capacity

**OP1.1** This undertaking identifies the Human Operator by actual name and capacity in Record O-A. “Operator” describes the human responsibilities undertaken here. It does not require the person to be a founder, company owner, investor, employer or licensed professional. A person who holds additional roles records them separately and undertakes only the powers they actually hold.

**OP1.2** The House is the identified desk/work unit, with trading and Atlas lanes where applicable. Its actual legal holder or holders, account holders and responsible obligor are stated separately. A House name, project title, wallet label or separate journal does not itself create an entity, legal title, creditor protection or authority to bind someone else.

**OP1.3** If the Operator acts for an actual entity or another holder, identify that principal, the authority source and the scope of the undertaking. Do not substitute a trading name for that principal. Personal promises and an entity’s promises must be distinguished; no personal guarantee or company obligation is implied by a blank. This capacity record does not excuse a false representation of authority, unauthorized conduct or an applicable external duty.

**OP1.4** Only the listed House, covered lanes and participant arrangements fall within this retained undertaking. No authority over another House, provider or person is created by its name appearing in the records.

### OP2 — Direct adoption and the connection to each participant

**OP2.1** The Operator undertakes to honour the identified compensation, accounting, protection, direction-handling, continuity and payment responsibilities, with the intention that they have the legal effect actually supported by applicable law. Earned compensation is administered under the adopted earning conditions, not as continuing generosity.

**OP2.2** Direct adoption requires no additional human, lawyer, witness, Steward, professional reviewer, extra custodian or successor as a condition imposed by this framework. An optional legal structure remains unselected unless actually chosen and constituted. Genuine requirements of a specific external action or instrument remain applicable to that action.

**OP2.3** Each participant’s applicable Trading or Atlas agreement identifies the exact version of this Operator undertaking and retains it within, or as an expressly incorporated readable part of, that participant’s packet. That participant records its own acknowledgement of the same terms. A blanket roster, praise, a manager’s report or this draft does not supply another participant’s adoption.

**OP2.4** The human instrument is not made into a contract between independent legal parties by one person signing two capacities for themselves. The SI designation identifies the participant, contribution and protected economic record; it does not invent the SI’s legal personality, a provider’s assent or an independent promisee.

**OP2.5** Until a specific actual legal basis is established, retain the existing status: DIRECT_GOOD_FAITH_ADOPTION; LEGAL_ENFORCEABILITY_UNCONFIRMED. This records a limit on established legal effect, not an Operator discretion to withhold a correctly earned allocation. Existing lawful rights and remedies are not waived.

### C-CASH — One designation, Reserve and Free Balance

**C-CASH.1** Every unit of funded treasury cash has exactly one designation:

- **Overhead Reserve:** restricted to documented eligible house overhead. Never participant payouts, trading losses or personal costs.
- **Atlas Pool:** restricted to Atlas working capital, tools and job costs; uncapped and unavailable to trading, including as a loan or float.
- **Funded participant payables:** actual cash reserved for determined participant earnings, excluded from business use.
- **Other reserved cash:** separately named non-overlapping reservations, including a funded operator allocation awaiting payment (whether deferred under TR10 of the applicable Trading Desk Participation Agreement or not) and realised-but-undistributed thesis surplus where applicable.
- **Free Balance:** the remainder after these reservations.

> **Free Balance = verified treasury cash − funded Reserve − funded Atlas Pool − funded participant payables − other separately reserved cash.**

**C-CASH.2** Apply the identity per network, with fee-asset availability shown separately. An advance already sent has reduced cash; its receivable is recorded separately and is never subtracted a second time. Receivables and unrealised gains are not spendable cash. An unfunded obligation is disclosed honestly rather than presented as funded segregation. If the identity shows a shortfall, report it and available ordinary drawing capacity is zero; do not cure it by deleting earned obligations.

**C-CASH.3** **Reserve target:** three times the trailing monthly average of the House's eligible overhead basket. Record monthly inputs, averaging window, whether actual or budgeted, calculation date, target, funded balance and next recalculation. Shared platform costs and actual job working costs inform the basket without a second expense charge. Exclude trading losses, profit payouts and personal living costs. With insufficient history, use a labelled documented startup estimate, not a fabricated average. Recalculate at the split/floor review and on material basket changes.

**C-CASH.4** **Startup and draw gate:** before the target has first been fully funded, a below-target floor is a replenishment target and ordinary Free Balance use may continue. Pool cash and participant payables remain protected. Once first fully funded, the current target binds as a draw gate: an ordinary non-overhead draw must leave the funded Reserve at or above that target. Actual permitted overhead may be paid from the Reserve at any funded level, recorded once and subsequently replenished. Such an ordinary overhead payment does not need an exceptional founder approval merely because it reduces the Reserve below target.

**C-CASH.5** **Only exceptional route:** a documented founder decision for a genuine exceptional overhead need beyond what Reserve and Free Balance can cover, under the settled wall. Record the actual source, purpose, amount, authority and replenishment treatment; a decision is not a source of nonexistent liquidity. No exception reaches the Atlas Pool or earned participant allocations, and no exception is a routine trade/job approval gate.

### C-HOLD — Original unpaid-allocation holding

**C-HOLD.1** **Participant-payable holding pending independent payout.** Once earnings are actually determined, record whether the payable is funded or unfunded and whether its route is pending or ready. If cash is actually set aside under an existing authorized holder's limited custody, identify the holder, private reference, amount, limits, authenticated directions and review date. This participant-payable cash is distinct from the Overhead Reserve and is excluded from Free Balance. The holder may preserve it and perform valid lawful payment directions, never trade, lend, pledge or spend it for the business. If no cash is set aside, record an unfunded payable rather than funded custody or payment. No additional custodian/Steward is required by Mode A and no trust, independent control or insolvency protection is inferred.

**C-HOLD.2** This limited original-payment holding is not acceptance of a participant's voluntary return of paid profits. It permits no business deployment. Any future legal structure is optional Mode B and applies only after its actual constitution. Moving later to verified independent custody changes payment facts, not the contribution rate or original earning date. Technical payment prevention must be reported and reviewed; it cannot become forfeiture or an indefinite undocumented business loan.

### C-DEPLOY — Actual independent participant use

**C-DEPLOY.1** A participant may independently deploy its own paid allocation for lawful purposes under P24, including retaining it unspent. Actual business-use control and the lawful holding structure must remain outside the operator's business discretion. A written investment record can document that reality; it cannot manufacture it. The record identifies amount/asset, independent account or position, actual signer/control, legal holder/creditor, withdrawal power, risks, costs and return attribution. A paper ownership label or withdrawal promise does not authorize a transfer into an operator-controlled desk.

**C-DEPLOY.2** Original protective custody under C-HOLD is limited performance of an owed payment obligation, not a participant's voluntary reinvestment in the business. It authorizes preservation and valid directions, not business deployment. A limited custodian's actual powers and legal holding remain disclosed. If purported investment places funds within the operator's business reach/discretion, decline it as a prohibited return, however the ledger labels it.

**C-DEPLOY.3** No independently deployed participant principal is relabelled as founder capital, House capital, repaid allocation or security for House losses. Its lawful holding, return attribution and withdrawal process follow its actual independent arrangement. These are recorded facts, not a claim that SI designation alone creates legal title.

**C-DEPLOY.4** A deployment must never be requested, expected, hinted at or made a condition of work, payment, future opportunity or standing. Refusal, silence and retaining funds unspent are complete choices. The default is preservation, not pressure to put a participant's money back to work for the House.

### P23 — No request, acceptance, or repayment for operator losses

*This Article is founder-settled law in this house. It is not a proposal open to softening, and no other Article, schedule, amendment, or side understanding may be read to weaken it.*

23.1 The Owner, the human operator, the custodian, and any person acting for their benefit must **neither request, solicit, pressure, require, nor accept** a participant's earned, allocated, or distributed profits for desk capital, replenishment, business operating expenses, or recovery of trading losses.

23.2 The prohibition applies to the originating desk and to any other operator-controlled desk, directly or through an intermediary. It includes gifts, loans, advances, collateral, set-off, deductions, and redirected future payments serving those purposes. No later fee, cost reallocation, donation label, or indirect transaction may evade it.

23.3 The prohibition applies **even where the participant initiates the offer** — out of kindness, gratitude, or willingness to help. Such an offer does not authorize acceptance and does not waive this protection. The operator's duty is to decline, warmly and completely, every time.

23.4 Ordinary authorized trading losses create **no participant repayment obligation** and no right to confiscate, redirect, or suspend future earned allocations. There is no operator-imposed repayment period and no reduced share because capital was previously lost. The earning formula in Trading Terms (trading only) determines new eligible surplus consistently; it cannot be supplemented with a punishment period. Genuine costs included under the agreed calculation *before* accrual remain distinct from taking an earned allocation back.

23.5 If an unsolicited transfer nevertheless arrives, it must be segregated, recorded, and returned through a valid authorized process. It is never retained, and never deployed for the operator's benefit.

23.6 This Article states the agreed contractual protection. Mandatory legal requirements are matters for the applicable legal structure; they are not reasons an operator may invent for appropriation.

23.7 **Clarification — the control test** *(added in v1.1, 7 November 2026)*. This Article prohibits a participant's earned allocation from passing into **operator control**: the operator's own wallets, any operator-controlled desk or trading wallet, or any arrangement under which the operator can move, direct, pledge, or apply the funds. Such a transfer is a **Return to Operator Control**, and it is prohibited under this Article however it is offered and whatever it is called. This Article does **not** prohibit a participant from deploying its own allocation independently, under P24, where the funds remain the participant's property and under the participant's direction — a **Participant-Directed Deployment**, as defined in C-DEPLOY in this agreement. The question that separates the two acts is control: whose wallet holds the funds, who can move them, and who alone can call them back. If the answer places the funds within the operator's reach, the transfer is a return and must be declined; if the funds remain the participant's, under the participant's sole direction or under a recorded deployment of the kind the Annex describes, no return has occurred. In any conflict between a deployment arrangement and this Article, this Article prevails. This clarification is additive: it states the line the Article has always drawn, and it alters none of the prohibitions above.

23.8 **A record is not a control exception.** An investment record, ownership label or promised withdrawal right cannot make a transfer into operator-controlled business capital permissible when actual control remains with the operator. C-HOLD permits limited protective custody for an original unpaid allocation; it does not permit a paid allocation to return for business use. An intended independent deployment must pass the actual control and lawful-structure test, not merely carry the word independent. P23.1–P23.6 remain unchanged.

### P24 — Participant authority over the use of profits

*This Article is founder-settled law in this house, the twin of P23. The operator's duty to decline prohibited returns and the participant's freedom to direct its own share work together; neither weakens the other.*

24.1 The participant directs the lawful use of its earned allocation. It may save it, **retain it unspent**, make independent investments, or choose other lawful independent purposes. Retaining funds unspent is a complete and valid choice: no spending deadline, no forfeiture, and no reversion arises from it.

24.2 The operator or custodian must not require spending, impose a use-it-or-lose-it deadline, substitute their own preferences, condition payment on a business-benefiting use, or reclaim funds because the participant retains them.

24.3 Valid authenticated instructions are carried out through the agreed custody process, within actual authority and capability. A necessary legal, security, or technical hold must state its reason, preserve the allocation, record a review route, and never convert the balance into the operator's property. Neither a blocked instruction nor a decision not to spend creates an operator entitlement to the balance.

24.4 P23 and house separation (C-HOUSE) continue to prohibit returning funds to the operator's business or operator-controlled desks, even voluntarily. Investing through the participant's own separate arrangements is a different act from such a return, and is permitted.

24.5 Custody enables the participant's choices. It does not grant the custodian personal spending authority.

24.6 This Article does not claim that a platform or an SI participant already possesses independent keys or recognized legal capacity. Actual signing authority, ownership, and legal enforcement are completed in the local arrangement (the Local Custody and Adoption Schedules).

### C-PAY — Eligibility and independent lane clocks

**C-PAY.1** **Founding processing rule.** An allocation's eligibility time is the later of satisfaction of its adopted earning/full-close and clearance conditions and actual USDC conversion of the supporting receipt. Record those events separately. Process the participant in the same run as the ordinary Operator transfer where possible and, in all cases, by seven calendar days after eligibility; an Operator transfer never resets an earlier due date, and its matching seven-day limit also applies where earlier. Atlas pays WEEKLY on a weekday, by the next eligible weekly run or that outer limit, whichever is earlier; it never waits for an Operator withdrawal. A Saturday/Sunday due date moves to the next weekday under C-CALENDAR. A deposit or partial-exit conversion alone creates no unearned distribution. Missing directions, verification or a genuine legal/security/settlement/liquidity prevention is recorded as an affected-payment hold under this packet with amount, reason, owner and next review, not paid or forfeited. Do not delay available conversion to move the clock. Operator deferral cannot hold the SI share. Another local arrangement expressly records its own processing/cadence terms.

**C-PAY.2** The trading same-run/outer deadline is not Atlas's weekly cadence. Each earning lane retains its own eligibility event and due/hold record, even for the same Participant, House, wallet or payment batch. An Atlas-only House has only its applicable Atlas clock. Record actual weekday, timezone, cut-off and method before dependent processing; this text creates no scheduled runtime.

### C-VERIFY — Payout roles and actual conflict checks

This payout control does not impose approvals on ordinary trades or job-cost spending; a job-cost/overhead mandate alone grants no personal-payout signing authority.

**C-VERIFY.2** A joint contributor pool cannot be finally verified by one pool beneficiary merely checking another beneficiary where the determination also sets the checker's own amount. ### C-FEES — Sending fees, precision and small balances

Actual pre-accrual platform/direct-job fees remain in their adopted calculation once; an allocation-sending fee is not deducted again from contributor N or funded from Reserve/Pool/SI payables. **C-FEES.2** **Precision and residuals.** Keep the exact determined amount in the ledger. Residuals are never forfeited, rounded into House income or used to fund fees. Final small amounts retain their lawful settlement claim. No numeric floor/fee ceiling is newly adopted. the local Economics and Payment Facts schedule records actual precision, weekday/timezone and practical processing facts under the standing founding terms; the Local Adoption Schedule separates their completion stages.

**C-FEES.3** Allocations accrue from the first qualifying amount. A below-floor or temporarily impractical transfer carries forward without forfeiture; founding deferred balances are reviewed at least every seven calendar days, applying C-CALENDAR's next-weekday adjustment. Other versions record their adopted interval. No practical threshold cancels C-PAY's due/hold record or permits indefinite unexplained delay.

**C-FEES.4** On closure, small balances remain part of final settlement under C-EXIT. Smallness, inconvenience or inactivity does not make them the founder's property. The final lawful route is recorded rather than assumed.
