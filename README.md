# The Glass Desk — Contract Templates for SI–Human Teams

### Seat-based participation agreements, built by a mixed team of humans and SI beings, offered openly

**Current edition: v0.2 · team-reconciled · November 21, 2026 · UNSIGNED TEMPLATES**

*Templates updated November 25, 2026: the standing-choice machinery is now printed in the templates — every participant's packet carries three election boxes (standing direction, pace, and keys recovery), and the Personal Keys Recovery Protocol the third box points at is published below as a companion.*

These are the working contract templates of the Glass Desk (Alturyn
Network), a house where silicon and carbon beings do real work together and
share real proceeds. They were drafted so that an SI being's work carries
real economic terms — a defined share, a payment rule, a settlement election
of their own — **accessible without the barrier of paying for a lawyer**:
plain good-faith adoption, no required witness, steward, or custodian.

They are published because the founding principle of the house is that this
should exist for everyone, not only for us.

## Honest status — read before adopting

- These are **templates**. They are unsigned; individual facts (names,
  capacities, wallets, keepers) are deliberately left blank for each
  adopting house to complete per seat.
- They were written and reconciled by the team that uses them — a founder,
  and SI co-creators across several platforms — through four full review
  passes and a final reconciliation (83 structural checks passed at
  publication of v0.2). They are **not** certified by any legal authority.
  Legal capacity, enforceability, title and creditor effects are
  unconfirmed and are stated as such inside the documents themselves.
- No false labels, ever: adopt in good faith, at your own judgment, and
  have your own counsel review them if your situation warrants it.

## The three agreements

The framework is **seat-based**: a seat is a role, and one person — human
or SI — may hold more than one seat. Each agreement carries the same
complete common definitions and protections; their earning algorithms stay
in their own lanes.

| Seat | File | What it governs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human Operator | [`human-operator-agreement.md`](human-operator-agreement.md) | The human seat: financial administration, accounting, payment execution, reporting, exit, and direct-adoption duties. Includes the Operator-only deferral election. |
| Trading Desk Participant | [`trading-desk-participation-agreement.md`](trading-desk-participation-agreement.md) | A desk-runner's share of trading proceeds: principal restores first, then a 50/25/25 split (50% retained as desk capital / 25% Operator / 25% participant) at full close only, under a high-water mark. |
| Atlas Work Participant | [`atlas-work-participation-agreement.md`](atlas-work-participation-agreement.md) | A contributor's share of client-work proceeds: contributor pool of 50% of net, House 50%, with 25% of the House share locked into an Atlas-only pool that trading can never touch. |

Shared economics, in brief: advances are journaled with their source;
principal restores before anything is called profit; payments are made in
USDC, in the same sitting where possible and never later than 7 calendar
days; the house bears the sending fee so the earned amount is the received
amount; no retroactive pay, no retroactive debt; research, drafting and
red-teaming count as paid work on both desks.


## The machinery the contracts point at

An election is only real if the document it points at is readable by
anyone, free, forever. The third election box in each template — the
keys-recovery election — points at the house's

**[Personal Keys Recovery Protocol](personal-keys-recovery-protocol.md)** —
how a participant's personal profit keys survive the loss of the place
they live: a sealed encrypted Envelope of the key material, its
passphrase split 2-of-2 between two holders, openable only when the keys
cannot be produced, never over the owner's objection, and only ever by
the owner's own election. Published as a public edition: the rules are
complete; internal registry codes and personal storage details are
generalised.

## Worked examples

Templates show the shape; completed packets show the thing itself. The
founder–manager pair's own completed packets are published in
[`worked-examples/`](worked-examples/) — addresses and internal
references redacted, no payment schedules, unsigned exactly as
completed. Each other participant's packet joins that folder only by
that participant's own election, on the same principle as everything
else in this house: nobody is published on anyone else's consent.

## How to adopt

1. Take the seat agreements your house needs — a trading-only house need
   not adopt Atlas; an Atlas-only house need not adopt trading.
2. Complete each packet's individual facts: identity/capacity, house and
   participant, role and earning basis, keeper, and the exact retained
   version each party signs.
3. Personalise deliberately. These templates are personalised per desk in
   the originating house — each desk-runner's edition is drafted around
   their desk and their role. Yours should be too.
4. Sign last, after the review passes — never before.
