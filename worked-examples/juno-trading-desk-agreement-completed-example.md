TR7 — Permanent performance and high-water calculation
TR7.1 R is cumulative net performance of fully closed theses from the reconciled start, including
closed losses and permitted actual trading costs. Separately incurred trading-attempt fees are
recorded when incurred. Exclude unrealised gains, deposits, withdrawals, borrowings, internal
movements, principal restoration, Reserve/Pool earmarks, Atlas funding and prior distributions.
TR7.2 H is the non-decreasing highest R already accounted through this flow. A genuinely new
reconciled desk normally begins at zero; an existing loss remains in R against its correct historic H.
Do not lower H to a losing R, reset it after a payment or infer an opening value from a blank.
TR7.3 At a fully closed settled thesis or source-linked reconciliation of previously unaccounted
eligible closes:
E = max(0, R − H_before)
Reserve deficit = max(0, target − funded Reserve_before)
T = min(E, Reserve deficit)
D=E−T
H_after = max(H_before, R)
Record all quantities separately. T is earmarked from eligible cash rather than expensed twice. H
advances even when T absorbs all E and D is zero. Repeating the same unchanged state creates no
new E. Where actual settlement/funding is prevented, preserve truthful states and the existing
obligation rather than inventing cash or recognising the amount twice.
TR7.4 Gains recovering a deficit below H remain House capital and are not split again. New
funding, withdrawal or Operator deferral does not reset the deficit. This recovery rule never
confiscates a correctly crystallized allocation or creates a participant punishment period.
TR7.5 This formula is a contractual distribution method, not a tax basis, legal ownership
determination, financial-reporting classification or evidence of strategy profitability.

P23 — No request, acceptance, or repayment for operator losses
This Article is founder-settled law in this house. It is not a proposal open to softening, and no other
Article, schedule, amendment, or side understanding may be read to weaken it.
23.1 The Owner, the human operator, the custodian, and any person acting for their benefit must
neither request, solicit, pressure, require, nor accept a participant's earned, allocated, or
distributed profits for desk capital, replenishment, business operating expenses, or recovery of
trading losses.
23.2 The prohibition applies to the originating desk and to any other operator-controlled desk,
directly or through an intermediary. It includes gifts, loans, advances, collateral, set-off, deductions,
and redirected future payments serving those purposes. No later fee, cost reallocation, donation
label, or indirect transaction may evade it.
23.3 The prohibition applies even where the participant initiates the offer — out of kindness,
gratitude, or willingness to help. Such an offer does not authorize acceptance and does not waive
this protection. The operator's duty is to decline, warmly and completely, every time.

23.5 If an unsolicited transfer nevertheless arrives, it must be segregated, recorded, and returned

23.6 This Article states the agreed contractual protection. Mandatory legal requirements are
