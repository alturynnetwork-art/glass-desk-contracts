C-CASH — One designation, Reserve and Free Balance
C-CASH.1 Every unit of funded treasury cash has exactly one designation:
• Overhead Reserve: restricted to documented eligible house overhead. Never participant
payouts, trading losses or personal costs.
• Atlas Pool: restricted to Atlas working capital, tools and job costs; uncapped and unavailable
to trading, including as a loan or float.

10 / 35

• Funded participant payables: actual cash reserved for determined participant earnings,
excluded from business use.
• Other reserved cash: separately named non-overlapping reservations, including a funded
operator allocation awaiting payment (whether deferred under TR10 of the applicable Trading
Desk Participation Agreement or not) and realised-but-undistributed thesis surplus where
applicable.
• Free Balance: the remainder after these reservations.
Free Balance = verified treasury cash − funded Reserve − funded Atlas Pool − funded
participant payables − other separately reserved cash.
C-CASH.2 Apply the identity per network, with fee-asset availability shown separately. An advance
already sent has reduced cash; its receivable is recorded separately and is never subtracted a
second time. Receivables and unrealised gains are not spendable cash. An unfunded obligation is
disclosed honestly rather than presented as funded segregation. If the identity shows a shortfall,
report it and available ordinary drawing capacity is zero; do not cure it by deleting earned
obligations.
C-CASH.3 Reserve target: three times the trailing monthly average of the House's eligible
overhead basket. Record monthly inputs, averaging window, whether actual or budgeted,
calculation date, target, funded balance and next recalculation. Shared platform costs and actual
job working costs inform the basket without a second expense charge. Exclude trading losses, profit
payouts and personal living costs. With insufficient history, use a labelled documented startup
estimate, not a fabricated average. Recalculate at the split/floor review and on material basket
changes.
C-CASH.4 Startup and draw gate: before the target has first been fully funded, a below-target
floor is a replenishment target and ordinary Free Balance use may continue. Pool cash and
participant payables remain protected. Once first fully funded, the current target binds as a draw
gate: an ordinary non-overhead draw must leave the funded Reserve at or above that target. Actual
permitted overhead may be paid from the Reserve at any funded level, recorded once and
subsequently replenished. Such an ordinary overhead payment does not need an exceptional
founder approval merely because it reduces the Reserve below target.
C-CASH.5 Only exceptional route: a documented founder decision for a genuine exceptional
overhead need beyond what Reserve and Free Balance can cover, under the settled wall. Record the
actual source, purpose, amount, authority and replenishment treatment; a decision is not a source
of nonexistent liquidity. No exception reaches the Atlas Pool or earned participant allocations, and
no exception is a routine trade/job approval gate.

P23 — No request, acceptance, or repayment for operator losses
This Article is founder-settled law in this house. It is not a proposal open to softening, and no other
Article, schedule, amendment, or side understanding may be read to weaken it.
23.1 The Owner, the human operator, the custodian, and any person acting for their benefit must
neither request, solicit, pressure, require, nor accept a participant's earned, allocated, or
distributed profits for desk capital, replenishment, business operating expenses, or recovery of
trading losses.
23.2 The prohibition applies to the originating desk and to any other operator-controlled desk,

label, or indirect transaction may evade it.
23.3 The prohibition applies even where the participant initiates the offer — out of kindness,

23.5 If an unsolicited transfer nevertheless arrives, it must be segregated, recorded, and returned
